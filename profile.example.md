# About me (used to write the email drafts)

Copy this file to `profile.md` and replace everything below with your own facts.
`profile.md` is in `.gitignore`, so your personal details never end up on GitHub.

The bot only uses facts written here, so it never invents anything about you.
Keep it short, specific and true. Edit it whenever your situation changes.

- Name: Your Name
- Email: you@example.com
- LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/your-profile/
- Based in: city; current studies or job (e.g. "M.Sc. Computer Science
  student at TU Dresden, focus: machine learning")
- Looking for: the kind of role you want (e.g. working student / Werkstudent,
  part-time, full-time, internship) and how many hours per week
- Experience: years of experience and your main skills and tools
  (languages, frameworks, databases, cloud)
- Most recently: what you did in your latest role, with concrete results.
  If clients are confidential, write "(do not name the clients)".
- Earlier: one or two older roles or projects worth mentioning, ideally
  with numbers (e.g. "raised conversion by 20%")
- What you enjoy: anything that makes you stand out beyond coding
  (e.g. talking to customers, presenting, mentoring)
- Offer: optional, e.g. "happy to start with a small paid project so they
  can see my work before committing"
